# Insights

Analysis only, no new runs: the evaluation rows under `results/<protocol>/`, under the frozen restart-v7 code (`scripts/cloudlab/code_state.py --check` passes on this tree). Model internals come from replaying each run's model on its per-episode dataset (`agent0.csv`, the agreed decisions and features) with the run's own prior and floor: predict on episode e's features, then update with the applied timeout and e's reward window, episode 1 never updated. The replay reproduces the live decision in every episode it was checked on (6 protocols x 6 model types, including PPO's sampled actions). One-off tooling: `scripts/cloudlab/tmp/cal/insights/` (not permanent).

Timing convention: episode e is applied at 20 e - 10 s on the client clock, and its 7 s feature window ends 2 s before that. So the first episode the dataset labels with a phase (k = 0) is applied at the onset but decided on the window just before it; the first decision made on post-onset data is k = 1. (`results/judge.py`'s `played()` assumes 20 k + 10, 20 s late; it only affects its displayed T column and the episodes CONVERGENCE uses for CV.)

## A. Priors before any online update

Each pretrained prior, freshly loaded (no online update), queried on the agreed feature vector of that configuration's latest draw: the last episode of p0 (healthy) and the first decision made on post-onset data after each onset (k = 1: the phase's first episode, k = 0, is applied at the onset but decided on the feature window just before it). GP: the prior's mean ± sd in reward units (before any data the sd is the kernel's prior sd, the same for every arm, so the pick is the argmax of the mean); the continuous GP is shown at the ladder rungs plus its own argmax over the grid. LinUCB: the prior estimate θ₀·x + bonus (the bonus is the same for every arm before data). Pick = what the learner would choose from that prior; the discrete LinUCB actually opens with a sweep over unplayed arms (+inf score), so its column is the prior-greedy choice. Arms the floor computed from that same window excludes are marked `x` (the GPs and the wrapper skip them).

## B. Untrained discrete GP in the first hold phase (p1)

Per decision k = 0..14 of p1 (k = 0 is decided on the pre-onset window), every draw: each arm's posterior mean ± sd and mean − sd in reward units, and n = the arm's observations so far (any context). `*` = chosen, `x` = excluded by the floor. Test: when the two best feasible means are within 0.05 (close), is the chosen arm the feasible arm with the most observations?
